## Founding chance by archetype (M4a review, placeholders)

Chance = habitability x site x supplies x colonists x natives x kit (percent factors, floored product, `src/shared/planetfall.ts`). Archetype names follow GAME_DESIGN; the site is a typical explored site (Explored survey band, site score 40 to 60). Targets from the review: an honest Ark about 60 to 75%, a reckless Scout about 15 to 30%.

## Headless harness (M7b, D-133)

- `tests/helpers/archetypes.ts`: six bots that play whole games through the command pipeline only (LaunchShip, FormExpedition, ExploreRegion, FoundColony, ReEmbark, RerouteShip, BuildDistrict, BuildSpaceport, SetColonyStance, ChooseTech, ResolveEventChoice, SetGameSpeed) and the integer sim. Ark-and-explore, Scout-first, Gambler, Economist (spaceport expander), Defender and Pusher (the same plan, different colony stance and landing party).
- `tests/helpers/balanceSweep.ts`: `runSweep(seeds, budgetMs)`, `aggregate`, `checkBands`, `renderReport`, and `BANDS`. Metrics per run: outcome, end day, colonies founded, founding success share (and first-roll share), population peak, ships lost, cards seen, score, projected real-time run length.
- `tests/balanceHarness.test.ts`: the default suite runs an 8-seed smoke sweep with loose bands plus recorded literals for seeds 1 to 8. `BALANCE_FULL=1` runs 40 seeds an archetype with the strict bands, `BALANCE_SEEDS=n` sets the count, `BALANCE_REPORT=1` writes `docs/BALANCE_REPORT.md`.
- Run length is projected, not played: speed 1x / 2x / 4x by state, plus think time (card 10 s, launch 40 s, landing 20 s, explore 5 s, found 10 s, build 5 s). Code constants outside data reach: `HOMEWORLD_CLOCK_DAYS` 8000 (was 5000, D-136), 1000 ms a day at 1x.
- Re-run after any formula or content change. The `balance-analyst` subagent owns this.

### M7b result (40 seeds an archetype, see BALANCE_REPORT.md)

- Wins: Ark 60%, Scout 50%, Gambler 45%, Economist 48%, Defender 43%, Pusher 45%. Best ratio 1.4x (limit 2x). Every archetype wins on at least one planet type. Ark founding at habitability 70+ is 73% (band 60-75%).
- Out of band, and why (asserted as known misses, not hidden):
  - Run length (fixed in M7c, D-136): it was 58-66 min with decisions, 82 min at pure 1x (band 90-150) with a 5000-day clock. It is now 92 min (Ark median) with the clock at 8000 days and the win rule at 10 colonies.
  - Gambler first-roll founding is 16% (band 25-45%): the bot launches into low supplies factors by design. Raising the food margin pushes its win rate to 83-88%, which breaks the competent band, so it was left.
- Structural findings: home-system planets are 0 days away (generator), founding retries are cheap (`failedLossPct` only hits the ground group), the armed bots are slightly weaker without natives, the Gambler is very sensitive to its food margin.
